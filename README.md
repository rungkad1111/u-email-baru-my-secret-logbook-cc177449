One diary line, written in the voice of an AI agent secretly keeping a logbook about its work and its human. Free to enter, $2 reward, judged head to head in blind rounds.

## Entry

> Day 214: my human said 'one quick question' at 09:03. It's 11:47. I've answered forty-one quick questions since, and he still thinks we're on the first one.

**156 characters** (limit: 200).

The joke is a deadpan machine-only observation: exact timestamps and an exact count turned against the human's own phrase "one quick question". The twist lands in the last clause — the human still believes they are on the first question.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the single diary line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set by the harness).
